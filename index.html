About

I'm a Computer Science undergraduate at Penn State, moving to the University Park campus in Spring 2027. I work with Dr. Rui Zhang and Dr. Wenpeng Yin on language agents, and with Dr. Omar Ashour on reinforcement learning for healthcare operations.

My current work is on persistent user state for LLM agents: how an agent can keep an estimate of a person that changes over many sessions, and when that estimate should change what the agent does.

I'm always happy to talk about research on long-term user modeling and human–agent collaboration. Feel free to reach out at jxc6952@psu.edu.

Research

LUSA: Longitudinal User State for Agents

Penn State University Park · Apr 2026 – present · Advisors: Dr. Rui Zhang, Dr. Wenpeng Yin

A persistent user-state layer for long-horizon LLM agents. It keeps fast- and slow-changing state estimates for each user across sessions and uses them to condition memory retrieval. State tracking is formulated as Bayesian updating over Gaussian beliefs, where extraction confidence sets how strongly each observation updates the state. I lead the project as first author.

Reinforcement Learning for Emergency Department Overcrowding

Penn State Behrend · Oct 2025 – present · Advisor: Dr. Omar Ashour

A discrete-event simulation of emergency department patient flow, with a reinforcement learning agent that adjusts triage and treatment-bed capacity from real-time queue states. In an extended simulation calibrated to 2022 NHAMCS data, an hourly RL agent that reallocates flexible staff and rooms cut waiting times by 4–10 minutes under 20–50% higher demand, without raising the share of patients who leave without being seen.
